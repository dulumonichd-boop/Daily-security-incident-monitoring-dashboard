Socio/political instability
Dhaka & Chattogram
 Low Just monitoring

Former Bangladeshi Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina, who was ousted from power and is currently facing a death penalty trial in Bangladesh, has announced plans to return to the country in December. She has called for the ban on her party, the Awami League, to be lifted, describing the charges and convictions against her as politically motivated and questioning the legitimacy of the February elections.

Bangladesh has continued to seek Hasina's extradition from India, where she is currently residing. Her proposed return could reignite political tensions, trigger protests by both supporters and opponents, and potentially strain India–Bangladesh relations. Given the legal proceedings against her and the country's polarized political environment, her return carries significant security risks, including the possibility of arrest or targeted violence.

The development comes at a time when Bangladesh has been showing signs of political and economic stabilization under the Bangladesh Nationalist Party (BNP) government led by Prime Minister Tarique Rahman. Any escalation linked to Hasina's return could challenge these gains and introduce fresh uncertainty into the country's political landscape.

👁 Anticipate ▲
🔗 Frontline 🔗 BBC 📎 Attach
Confirmation of a specific December return date, India's response to extradition requests, and any signs of mobilisation by Awami League supporters or opponents ahead of the announced return.
